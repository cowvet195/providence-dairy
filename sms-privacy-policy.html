SMS Privacy Policy

This policy covers Providence Dairy Inc.’s office-assistant text messaging program only.

The program uses mobile numbers, message contents, and consent and opt-out records to coordinate operations, answer inquiries, send reminders, and provide call-status updates. We do not send marketing texts.

Providence Dairy Inc. will not sell or share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We will not share your opt-in to this SMS program with any third party for purposes unrelated to providing you with the services of this program. We may share your SMS opt-in or consent status with providers that help deliver these text messages, including platform providers and phone companies, or as required by law.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe, START to resubscribe, or HELP for help. For privacy questions, email paul@prv.farm.
